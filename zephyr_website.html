What Zephyr can do

Everything your
community needs.

Built from the ground up for real Discord servers — not just a template. Every feature works together.

Security

Captcha verification, raid protection, account age gating, scam link auto-bans, and anti-spam. Your server stays clean without any effort.

Leveling

Members earn XP every message with a cooldown system. Level-up announcements and coin bonuses keep engagement high.

Economy

A full coin economy with daily rewards, a shop with collectibles, leaderboards, and player-to-player transfers.

Music

Play YouTube audio directly in voice channels. Full queue system with skip, pause, resume, and stop controls.

Mini-games

Coinflip, slots, rock paper scissors, and trivia — all with coin rewards. Keep your community entertained and engaged.

Starboard

Messages that earn enough ⭐ reactions automatically get pinned to your starboard channel. The best moments, preserved.

Role menus

Post a dropdown menu where members can self-assign and remove roles in one click. No mod required.

Tickets & modmail

Members open private support tickets with a button click. DMs to Zephyr get forwarded to your modmail channel.

Polls & giveaways

Run polls with up to 4 options and timed giveaways that auto-pick winners — all with one slash command.
